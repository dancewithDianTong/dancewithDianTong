# Hi, I'm Diantong Li 👋

I'm interested in **Bayesian optimization, probabilistic machine learning, and decision-making under uncertainty**.

My research focuses on efficient learning and optimization methods for complex black-box problems, with interests spanning Gaussian processes, multi-objective optimization, meta-learning, and scientific applications of machine learning.

### 🔬 Research Interests

- **Bayesian Optimization** — constrained and multi-objective optimization, acquisition functions, and sample-efficient decision-making
- **Probabilistic Machine Learning** — Gaussian processes, kernel methods, and uncertainty quantification
- **Meta-Learning** — transferable surrogate models and learning across optimization tasks
- **Machine Learning for Science** — molecular design and drug optimization
